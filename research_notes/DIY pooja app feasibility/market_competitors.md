# Online Pandit / Pooja / Devotional Services Apps: Market and Competitors (India and Diaspora)

Method note: compiled 2026-10-21. Page fetches (inc42, entrackr, techcrunch) failed with DNS errors in this environment, so every finding below comes from search-result snippets and summaries. Treat the figures as press-reported and mostly company-claimed. No source found was dated after mid-2026. Dates are given where the source shows them.

## 1. Market size, growth, diaspora demand

### Takeaway
Estimates of the overall Indian faith/spiritual economy run from about $40–60B and grow at roughly 9–10% CAGR. The online-addressable slice (e-puja and online spiritual services) is much smaller, about INR 4,000–5,000 Cr/yr (~$0.5–0.6B) and growing 15–18%. Diaspora users are a small share of users but bring in an outsized share of revenue.

### Cited Findings
- IMARC put India's religious/spiritual market at about $54B in 2022, projected above $97B by 2028 — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/explainers/indias-love-for-religion-astrology-births-35-billion-industry)
- $58.6B in 2024 at about 10% CAGR, projected above $151.9B by 2034 (Inc42 brand-partner piece on VAMA, so promotional) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/how-vama-is-tapping-into-indias-58-6-bn-spiritual-tech-market/)
- $58.56B as of 2023 at 8.82% CAGR — [The Growth Loop](https://thegrowthloop.beehiiv.com/p/how-appsforbharat-grows)
- An NSSO-based estimate puts the Hindu temple economy at about INR 3.02 trillion (~$40B), about 2.3% of GDP — [TechCrunch, Jun 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/sri-mandir-keeps-investors-hooked-as-digital-devotion-grows)
- One estimate puts the devotion economy at about $44B, of which temples are about $27B (AppsForBharat careers page, company source) — [Kula](https://careers.kula.ai/appsforbharat/51985)
- ePuja Spiritech's filing: online spiritual services worth INR 4,000–5,000 Cr per year, growing 15–18%; spiritual products market INR 7,000–8,000 Cr — [IndiaInfoline MD&A](https://www.indiainfoline.com/company/epuja-spiritech-ltd/management-discussions)
- India's incense market alone is about $1.23B at about 6% CAGR; there are 950+ faith-tech startups in India — [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/puja-brands-raise-festive-marketing-spend-betting-up-to-rs-2-5-crore-per-state-on-devotional-demand-80166.htm)
- MAU across India's top-10 religious apps grew 60% in H1 2025, against a 15% rise globally — [TechCrunch, Jun 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/sri-mandir-keeps-investors-hooked-as-digital-devotion-grows)
- Diaspora at Sri Mandir: about 20% of revenue comes from the diaspora (US, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, NZ), from about 90K of 3.5M MAU (~2.6%). ARPU abroad is about INR 7,000 (~$81) against INR 600–800 in India. The core diaspora users are first- and second-generation Indian Americans — [TechCrunch, Jun 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/sri-mandir-keeps-investors-hooked-as-digital-devotion-grows). Another TechCrunch piece cites about $100 average monthly spend outside India, with a user calling the app "ultra-premium" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2851321)

### Inferences
- Headline TAM figures ($40–150B) mostly cover offline temple donations, travel and goods. The realistic digital SAM is the ~INR 4–5K Cr online-services figure plus a share of the INR 7–8K Cr products market.
- Diaspora ARPU is roughly 9–12x India ARPU. That makes the diaspora the most attractive segment for a paid DIY or guided product.

### Gaps
- I found no independent (non-company) sizing of the diaspora devotional-services market in the US, UK, Canada or Gulf.
- Different methods produce very different market sizes. None of the sources is a rigorous primary study.

## 2. Key players

### Takeaway
Sri Mandir (AppsForBharat) is the funded leader in e-puja and chadhava. AstroTalk is far larger by revenue, but its revenue comes from astrology, with puja as a side line. VAMA, Utsav and DevDham are smaller, VC-backed challengers. Pandit-booking marketplaces (SmartPuja, 99Pandit) are small or opaque.

Total funding is about $50–53M — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/appsforbharat-raises-20-mn-in-series-c-round-9451578), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=521064), [TechCrunch, Jun 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/sri-mandir-keeps-investors-hooked-as-digital-devotion-grows)

- 3.5M MAU, 30+ countries, 100+ temples, about 1.2 lakh paid users (founder claim). Other coverage cites 1.2M users doing 5.2M online pujas or offerings across 70+ temples in 12 months. Six-month retention is about 55% — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/startups/can-appsforbharat-build-the-uber-or-airbnb-for-indias-spiritual-tech-market/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/sri-mandir-keeps-investors-hooked-as-digital-devotion-grows)

## 3. Revenue models and ARPU

### Takeaway
Transactional e-puja and chadhava bookings, often pooled group pujas with high margins, are the proven monetizer for pure-play devotional apps. Paid live consultation monetizes far better but belongs to astrology. Products and samagri form a growing second leg. I found no evidence that subscriptions or ads drive meaningful revenue.

### Cited Findings
- Sri Mandir: revenue comes mainly from booked pujas and chadhava, with group ceremonies pooling devotees. ARPU is INR 600–800 in India and about INR 7,000 abroad; 20% of revenue comes from the diaspora — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/sri-mandir-keeps-investors-hooked-as-digital-devotion-grows)
- Implied: INR ~70 Cr / ~1.2 lakh paid users ≈ INR ~5,800 per paid user per year (derived from the Inc42 figures above; rough)
- AstroTalk: about 90% from per-minute consultations; e-commerce (products) at INR 200 Cr ARR — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/explainers/indias-love-for-religion-astrology-births-35-billion-industry), [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/astrotalks-e-commerce-vertical-posts-rs-140-cr-revenue-in-2025-hits-rs-200-cr-arr-11004745)
- Utsav: order-volume model (about 2.5 lakh orders per month) covering pujas and prasad — [Indian Retailer](https://indianretailer.com/news/funding-alert-kolkata-based-spiritual-platform-utsav-raises-rs-36-cr-series-round)
- Subscription example: I-Purohith (iOS DIY guide) offers about $9.99 per year for premium poojas — [AppsHunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/i-purohith/id6751193373)

### Inferences
- Ranked by evidence of monetization: (1) live paid consults; (2) e-puja and chadhava bookings; (3) product and samagri e-commerce; (4) subscriptions and ads, which are unproven at scale in this category.
- A DIY app could pair a free or cheap guide with upsells for samagri kits and "book a pandit live". The best target is diaspora users, where ARPU is roughly 10x.

### Gaps
- No published samagri-kit margins or subscription conversion rates were found.
- No ad revenue data for devotional apps was found.

## 4. Existing self-guided DIY pooja apps

### Takeaway
Several small apps offer step-by-step home puja guidance. None of them appears to have scale, funding or strong reviews. The major players focus on done-for-you pujas rather than DIY, so the DIY niche looks under-served but already has some entrants.

### Cited Findings
- Ghar: Hindu Home Rituals (iOS): step-by-step guided puja and aarti flows, japa counters, Hindi and English — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ghar-hindu-home-rituals/id6760098094)
- Karmakanda: puja steps for household and temple rituals, with translations, meanings and a materials list per ritual — [mwm.ai listing](https://mwm.ai/de/apps/karmakanda/6753807702)
- I-Purohith (iOS): a "digital priest" with step-by-step audio; premium poojas cost about $9.99 per year — [AppsHunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/i-purohith/id6751193373)
- Ganesh Puja and Aarti: free, offline, includes a visarjan guide; one review reports it stuck on loading — [AppsHunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/1435729464)
- Single-festival Android guides exist, such as Puja Vidhi in Hindi – Laxmi Pujan — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/lifestyle/5-must-have-apps-to-elevate-your-diwali-celebration)
- Some services are a hybrid in which a pandit demonstrates over video and the devotee follows step by step — [Asian Age](https://www.asianage.com/life-and-style/apparent-way-pray-315)

### Inferences
- The DIY apps found are recent (high iOS IDs such as id675x and id676x suggest launches in 2025–26) or single-festival apps, with no visible scale. Their quality is uncertain and could not be checked hands-on.
- One thing appears missing: a comprehensive multi-puja, multi-tradition DIY guide integrated with samagri ordering and a live-pandit fallback. I found no evidence of such a product.

### Gaps
- No download counts, ratings or hands-on quality checks of the DIY apps (fetch failed).
- I did not confirm whether Sri Mandir, VAMA or Utsav ship any in-app DIY vidhi feature.
